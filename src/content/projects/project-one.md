## Overview

Pyxis presents a connected digital platform for hospitality operations. Its public website organizes property management, channel management, and booking around a clear operational promise: “The smarter way to run your hotel.”

## Product Areas

- Property Management System for hotel operations.
- Channel management for reservation distribution.
- Booking tools for guest-facing reservations.

## Public Signals

The live website states that Pyxis is trusted by more than 900 hotels and restaurants and integrates with more than 50 partners.
