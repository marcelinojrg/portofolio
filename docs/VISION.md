# Website Vision

## 1. Project Statement

Create a premium personal portfolio website for Marcelino Jorgi that communicates identity, capability, curiosity, and craft through a combination of typography, visual storytelling, interaction, and motion.

The website should feel like an experience that is worth exploring, not a document that is merely scanned.

## 2. Positioning

Primary positioning:

**Information Systems Student + Creative Technologist + Digital Product Builder**

This positioning should remain flexible enough to evolve as Marcelino gains more professional experience.

The site should communicate that the background in Information Systems supports an ability to understand systems, data, technology, products, and users while also having an interest in visual design and digital experiences.

## 3. Audience

Primary audiences:

- Recruiters
- Internship coordinators
- Potential employers
- Project collaborators
- Academic contacts
- People discovering Marcelino through social media or search

Secondary audience:

- Other students and early-career technologists interested in how projects are built

## 4. User Questions

A visitor should be able to answer these questions quickly:

1. Who is Marcelino?
2. What does he build or work on?
3. What are his strongest areas?
4. What projects demonstrate those abilities?
5. How can someone contact him?

A deeper visitor should also be able to understand:

- The problems behind selected projects.
- Marcelino's role.
- The process used.
- The technology used.
- What was learned.

## 5. Experience Goal

The experience should feel:

- Premium
- Calm
- Confident
- Modern
- Editorial
- Cinematic
- Curious
- Technical without feeling cold
- Experimental without feeling chaotic

## 6. Primary Reference Direction

Primary visual and interaction reference:

https://mimo.xiaomi.com/

Use the reference for principles such as:

- Large-scale visual hierarchy
- Strong typography
- Product-like storytelling
- Spatial composition
- Scroll-based pacing
- Smooth transitions
- Controlled visual effects
- Clear sectional rhythm

Do not copy:

- Brand identity
- Logos
- Images
- Product copy
- Proprietary UI
- Exact section structures
- Exact animations
- Source code

The goal is to learn from the quality of presentation, not reproduce the reference.

## 7. Storytelling Model

The homepage should behave as a narrative:

### Act 1: Identity

Introduce Marcelino in a visually strong way.

### Act 2: Direction

Explain what he cares about and what he builds.

### Act 3: Proof

Show selected work and case studies.

### Act 4: Depth

Reveal experience, capabilities, tools, or selected personal interests when useful.

### Act 5: Invitation

End with a confident but simple contact section.

## 8. Homepage Intent

A recommended sequence is:

1. Navigation
2. Hero
3. Short positioning statement
4. Selected work introduction
5. Project showcase sequence
6. About or philosophy section
7. Experience / capabilities
8. Contact statement
9. Footer

The exact order may change during visual prototyping if the resulting narrative becomes stronger.

## 9. Hero Philosophy

The hero should not look like a standard template with:

`Hello, I am [Name]` + button + profile picture.

Instead, it should immediately establish a visual world and a point of view.

A strong direction is:

- Large display typography
- A short, precise statement
- A strong visual field
- Minimal controls
- Carefully choreographed entrance motion

The hero should create curiosity without hiding the core identity.

## 10. Project Philosophy

Selected work is the proof section.

Each project should communicate:

- Context
- Challenge
- Role
- Thinking
- Craft
- Technology
- Outcome, only when evidence exists

Avoid presenting every project equally. The homepage should curate a small number of strong examples.

## 11. Visual Personality

The site should have a recognizable visual language even without its name or logo.

The personality should come from:

- Typography
- Spacing
- Motion
- Image treatment
- Section transitions
- Composition
- Consistent interaction behavior

## 12. What the Website Must Not Become

Do not allow the site to drift into:

- Generic AI portfolio aesthetics
- A resume with animation added afterward
- A grid of identical cards
- A dashboard-like layout
- A component showcase
- A visual effects demo
- A content-heavy blog unless deliberately expanded later

## 13. Success Criteria

The website succeeds when:

- The first viewport immediately establishes identity.
- The page feels coherent while scrolling.
- Motion feels deliberate rather than excessive.
- Projects are memorable.
- The visual system feels custom.
- Content remains easy to understand.
- Mobile still feels designed rather than merely responsive.
- Performance remains strong despite rich interaction.
